What this says

    How to read this

    • Time to merge counts merged PRs only, from opened to merged. PRs that expired or failed CI are not in it, which is why the expired share sits beside it.
    • Stages: waiting for CI, then waiting for a reviewer to open it, then the fuse (the window to reject). The comparison bar has no stages because ordinary PRs do not record them.
    • Expired unopened means nobody looked before the PR's lifetime ended, so it was deleted: the review queue, made visible.
    • Groups under 3 PRs are hidden so no one can be singled out. There is no per-person view, on purpose.
    • The baseline is a comparison, not proof: teams, seasons and PR sizes differ. Small samples (under about 30 PRs) are hints.

    Load a repository, or look at the demo

    Enter owner/repo and press Load. The dashboard reads outcomes.json from the repository's snapgit-metrics branch (written by the snapgit Action, or published by snapgit metrics --publish) and compares it with that repository's ordinary closed PRs. Everything is read in your browser, straight from the GitHub API.
